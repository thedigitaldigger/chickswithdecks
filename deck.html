Four suits, one deck

Everything we do belongs to one of four suits. Most briefs need a hand with all four in it.

Espadas Strategy

The cut. We find the one thing a brand can say that nobody else can, and cut everything else.

  • Brand and business strategy
  • Positioning
  • Research that asks the awkward question
  • Launch planning
  • The one-card brief

Bastos Craft

The making. Identity, film and design, made by hand where it matters, by people who still enjoy making them.

  • Brand identity
  • Film and photography
  • Design and art direction
  • Social and content
  • Production

Copas Culture

The celebration. Campaigns people bring up over dinner, made with the communities they belong to.

  • Campaigns
  • Entertainment and formats
  • Music and sound
  • Events and experiences
  • Community and talent partnerships

Oros Value

The gold. Products, commerce and growth, and the numbers that prove the rest of the hand worked.

  • Product and innovation
  • Commerce and retail
  • Growth and performance
  • Measurement
